The confirmed web feature block is eight tiles: one image-only, one intro line, six benefits. FeatureTile is that cell.

```jsx
<FeatureTile title="Universal outlets" body="That work with UK, US, and EU plugs — no adapters, no guessing." />
<FeatureTile image="assets/renders/hero/omu-3x-hero-olive-r03.png" />
```

Omitting `title` renders an image-only tile. Two tones only: `tile` (light grey) and `dark` (the Black plane).
