Renders the supplied OMU logo SVGs — use it anywhere a mark is needed instead of typesetting or drawing one.

```jsx
<Logo mark="wordmark" tone="dark" height={28} assetPath="../assets/logo" />
```

The light SVGs use palette White (#D7D2CB). Clear space: at least the height of the `o`. In body copy the brand name is always `OMU` in caps; lowercase belongs to the mark only.
