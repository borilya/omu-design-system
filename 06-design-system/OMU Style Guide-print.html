Design System Style Guide

Brand platform

The quiet glow of a good find.

OMU makes extension cords, power strips, cables and accessories for the UAE market, positioned as interior objects rather than electronics — things you leave out on the counter next to the lamp and the vase.

Audience: affluent Dubai expats, mostly men 28–45, $10k+ income, who keep tuning their homes and won't tolerate cheap plastic on display.

One warm you, never "the consumer." A full stop instead of an exclamation mark, nearly always. Sentence case everywhere except labels and the name OMU. Short sentences. Emoji: effectively none.

Cards & surfaces

A card is a flat plane — light grey tile, 20px radius, no border, no shadow, contents padded 32px. Where separation is needed, a 1px hairline of Black at 16%. Shadows are diffuse and shallow — nothing glossy, no glass, no neon, no gradients as decoration.

Motion

220ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.6,.2,1). Fades and small position shifts only — no bounce, no spring. Hover: opacity fade to ~0.78, or border darkening. Press: no shrink or scale — state is carried by fill. Focus: border goes to Black, no glow.

Imagery

Two registers, never mixed.

StudioObject on white or transparent, soft diffuse shadow, neutral top light, 3/4-from-above angle. The main device: cord laid out in concentric coils around the body, plug set apart — the recognisable brand silhouette. No captions, callouts, icons or badges over a studio frame.
InteriorObject small and off-centre in a real home — books, a lamp, a throw. Warm soft light, long blurred shadows. Natural materials: oak, marble, travertine, leather, velvet, wool. No people. Existing frames are vertical only.

Colour vibe: warm, low-contrast, matte. Cool sterile showroom lighting and magazine gloss are out of bounds.

Iconography

There is no OMU icon system — no icon font, no SVG sprite. Instead: the supplied logo SVGs; typographic marks in place of icons (the Select caret is two 6px triangles drawn in CSS); product-side marking is laser-etched engraving, not iconography. Emoji are not used.

Hard prohibitions

Any one of these makes the material not-OMU.

  • Bright or blinking LEDs, especially blue
  • Glossy highlights, gradients, glass and neon effects
  • Office/techno aesthetics and aggressive engineering
  • Sterile showroom or magazine gloss in interior frames
  • The product hidden, cropped, or shown without its cord
  • Callouts, icons, badges or promo plates over a studio frame
  • Exclamation marks, stop words, scattered emoji
  • Cyrillic in brand communication
  • The words socket / outlet device / wiring
  • The brand name written "Omu" or "omu" in running text
